    04 · CONNECT THE IDEAS

    What does the mathematics predict?

    First use your experiments to make a claim about the center and spread. Then reveal the theory. Revisit your opening prediction after the experiments.

    Center of sample means
    E(X̄) = μ = 8

    Averages are centered at the population mean.

    Standard deviation of sample means
    SD(X̄) = σ / √n

    For this population: 8 / √n minutes.

    For n = 1, the predicted standard deviation is 8.00 minutes.

    Across our three sample sizes: n = 1 → SD = 8.00; n = 4 → SD = 4.00; n = 36 → SD ≈ 1.33 minutes.

    Central Limit Theorem: For independent, identically distributed observations with finite variance, the distribution of the sample mean approaches a normal distribution as n increases. This does not mean the original population becomes normal.
